How to play

On a computer

WASD or Arrow keys — Move

DRAGON BATTLE CONTROLS

WASD / Arrow Keys — Move

SPACE — Swing sword

SHIFT + Direction — Dodge roll

Watch for warning markers before the dragon attacks!

Time your dodges carefully. They have a short cooldown.

MOBILE CONTROLS

Sword Button — Attack during combat
